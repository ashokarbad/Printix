About Printix

We print personalised T-shirts, mugs, photo frames and caps for gifts, teams and small businesses.

Printix turns your photos, names and logos into things people use and keep.

A T-shirt for a birthday, a mug for a colleague, a framed photo for a parent, caps for a cricket team. Small things that mean something because they carry your picture or your words.

We print single pieces and bulk orders. Most orders start with a WhatsApp message and a photo, and end with something you're happy to give or wear.

How we work

What you approve is what we print.

  • We check your file first

    If a photo is too small or blurry to print well, we tell you before you pay and suggest what will work.

  • You approve a preview

    We send a preview of your design on the product. We print only after you say yes.

  • Prices agreed up front

    You know the full price before you pay, and bulk orders get a quote first.

  • Your photos stay private

    We use your designs only for your order and don't show them anywhere without your permission.

Who we print for

One piece or a hundred, the process is the same.

  • Gifts and occasions

    Birthdays, anniversaries, weddings and festivals. One mug or one frame is enough.

  • Teams and events

    Matching T-shirts and caps for sports teams, college fests, rallies and office events.

  • Shops and offices

    Logo polos for staff, caps for promotions and mugs for clients.
