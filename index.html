    How ordering works

    Nothing goes to print until you have seen it and said yes.

    1. Fill your cart

      Add the products you want, with size, colour and quantity, then send the cart on WhatsApp.

    2. Send your design

      Share your photo, logo or text on WhatsApp. We check that it will print clearly.

    3. Approve the preview

      We send you a preview and the final price. Ask for changes until it looks right.

    4. Pay and we print

      We share payment details on WhatsApp once you approve. Printing starts when the payment reaches us.
