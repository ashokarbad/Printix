Privacy

In short: We use your details and designs only for your order.

What we collect

  • Your name and mobile number, and your email if you give it.
  • The photos, logos and text you send for printing.
  • Order details: products, sizes, quantities, payment reference and delivery details.

How we use it

We use this information only to prepare, print, deliver and support your order, and to reply to your questions. We don't sell it or share it with anyone for marketing.

WhatsApp and payments

Orders and quotes are sent through WhatsApp, and payments go through your own payment app or bank. Those services handle your data under their own privacy policies.

Your designs

We don't show your photos or designs to anyone, or use them as examples of our work, without your permission. We keep order files for [number of days or months — confirm] so we can reprint if needed, then delete them. Ask us on WhatsApp to delete them sooner.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Forms on this site don't store anything; they open WhatsApp with your message filled in.

Terms and conditions

In short: You approve a preview; we print exactly what you approved.

  • By sending us a design, you confirm you have the right to print it. We may refuse designs that copy someone else's brand or artwork, or that are offensive or unlawful.
  • We send a preview for approval. Once you approve it, we print exactly what was approved, so please check names, spellings and dates carefully.
  • Printed colours can look slightly different from your screen, and photos taken on phones may print softer than they look. We tell you if we expect a problem.
  • Product pictures on this website are illustrations. Your preview on WhatsApp shows your actual design.
  • Prices on this website are starting prices. The final price is confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay.
  • Typical production time is [number of working days — confirm] after payment and design approval. Bulk orders may take longer; we confirm the date when you order.

Payments and refunds

In short: Full refund if you cancel before printing starts.

How to pay

After you approve your preview, we send you the final amount and payment details on WhatsApp. [Accepted payment methods, e.g. UPI, cash at the shop — confirm]

Your order is confirmed once the payment reaches us. A screenshot on its own doesn't confirm a payment, so we check our account before we start printing.

We never ask for your UPI PIN, OTP, card number or bank login. If anyone does, it isn't us.

Cancellations

You can cancel for a full refund before we start printing. After printing has started, personalised items can't be cancelled because they can't be resold.

Refunds and reprints

  • If an item arrives damaged, or doesn't match the preview you approved, tell us within [number of days — confirm] with photos. We will reprint it or refund you.
  • We can't refund items that match the approved preview, including spelling mistakes that were in the approved design.
  • Refunds go back to the account you paid from, within [number of working days — confirm] of our approving the refund.
